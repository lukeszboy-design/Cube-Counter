A calculator that shows the math

Cube Counters! is a free calculator for kids. Every number is a friendly robot carrying that many counting cubes, so children can see what a number means instead of just reading it. A cheerful voice reads each number and answer out loud.

Watch the math happen

Robots leap together to add. Cubes pop away to take away. Robots line up in equal groups to multiply, and a robot bursts into equal groups to divide. Decimals are drawn at their true size, so 0.5 is half a cube.

Practise with Challenge mode

Levels cover adding to 10, adding and taking away to 20, multiplying as groups, dividing by sharing, and missing-number puzzles like 5 + ? = 8, with hints and an optional 5-second timer. Right answers earn stars that unlock hats, robot colors and birds, and kids can design their own robot in Build a Robot.

For parents and teachers

Cube Counters! is free, with no ads, accounts or sign-up. It runs in any web browser on computers, Chromebooks and tablets, and there are free desktop apps for Windows and Mac on the download page.
